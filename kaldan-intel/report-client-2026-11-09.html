3Analytics: the site already sends data — it just isn't being used

The website already runs Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. Conversion actions (booking start, room-page views, offer clicks) are not yet organised into a measured funnel, and no behaviour-recording tool is installed. This means today nobody can answer — from data — questions like:

  • How many site visitors actually reach the booking engine? Where do they drop off?
  • Which channel — Instagram, Google, OTAs — delivers guests at the best margin?
  • What does a visitor do on mobile before abandoning the "Book Now" journey?

Opportunity: configure the existing tags into a weekly booking funnel, add Microsoft Clarity (free) for heatmap & session insight, and tag every campaign with consistent UTM links. First measurable funnel baseline within ~2 weeks of access.

4Search & content (SEO)

  • Technical base is sound: clean sitemap, open robots policy, canonical tags, structured data (Hotel + 8 room types with location coordinates) already in place.
  • Stale content is the visible issue: expired event posts and dated blog titles remain live; a refresh calendar for the highest-intent posts (destination guides, wedding planning, ECR itineraries) is quick, low-cost work.
  • Missing structured-data types: FAQ and Event/Offer markup can be added so Kaldan qualifies for richer Google results — answer boxes, event cards, offer badges.

5AI search visibility (new channel, zero competitors measuring it)

Travellers increasingly ask AI assistants ("best luxury resort in Mahabalipuram…") instead of scrolling results. Kaldan's site is already technically readable by these systems — the llms.txt file proves early intent. What no one has measured is whether that investment works.

Opportunity: a monthly AI Mention Scoreboard — Kaldan vs. named competitors across five typical traveller questions on major AI assistants. Benchmark it once, then track monthly. Kaldan would be the first hotel in the market measuring this systematically.

7Revenue opportunities

  • Direct-booking margin: Kaldan's own booking engine exists on its own domain. A monthly price-parity audit across all 6 live deal listings (₹23,216–₹32,657 band, measured 09 Nov) confirms the best rate is always on the direct channel — each booking moved from OTA to direct keeps the commission in house.
  • Weddings as a measured segment: a dedicated weddings microsite section + Instagram "Weddings" highlight suggest this is a priority revenue line; a proper enquiry-tracking path would show leads by source for the first time.
  • Offer-page focus: eight concurrent offers compete on one page; test one hero offer per season and measure the click-through change.
  • Review multiplication: 2,702 reviews at 4.9 is world-class social proof — turning it into on-page review markup, short video reels and on-property QR cards compounds it.

8Proposed 90-day engagement

Month 1 — Instrument & audit Set up measurement: booking funnel dashboard, behaviour analytics, blog/content audit, AI-mention baseline, Instagram insights baseline, price-parity audit. Deliverable: baseline vs. everything re-measured monthly.
Month 2 — Execute & compound Ship the fixes: content refreshes, YouTube metadata uplift, Reddit/Quora participation, wedding lead tracking, offer A/B test, review markup live.
Month 3 — Prove & extend Show movement: funnel week-on-week changes, AI-visibility delta, direct-booking-engine share, review velocity. Each monthly report: (a) baseline vs. today, (b) what moved, (c) what's next.
