3The two measurable deltas holding the rank

  • Perceived property freshness: "Room tired", "1970's", "worn" appear across recent reviewer samples, matching the Rooms 4.0 and Value 4.0 sub-scores. Selected room re-imaging and refresh cycles are visible to reviewers — updated room photography alone measurably changes conversion on booking platforms.
  • Function noise vs. room experience: multiple guests mention wedding/conference sound carrying into rooms, and advise future guests to request rooms away from event spaces. A low-cost room-assignment policy change turns a recurring complaint into a solved experience.

4Analytics: the resort has flown blind for three years

The website's only measurement tag is a Google Analytics property (UA-108056697-1) that stopped recording in 2023. There is no Tag Manager, no GA4, no behavioural recording, and no data since. This means questions like the following currently have no answer at the resort:

  • How many visitors reach the booking engine, and where do they leave?
  • Which channel — Instagram, Google search, or OTAs — actually delivers bookings?
  • What do mobile visitors see first, and does the 15KB homepage even load its imagery before they judge it?

Opportunity: install Tag Manager + GA4 + Microsoft Clarity (free) with a consent-first setup, rebuild the booking funnel, and give the resort its first real visitor data within two weeks.

5The website rebuild (proposed)

  • Fix the broken storefront: a fast, modern site in the resort's own voice — beach heritage since 1978, 12 acres of garden meeting a private beach — with every room category, dining venue and offer properly presented per-page.
  • Bring the booking home: connect the existing SwiftBook engine behind the resort's own domain experience, with price-parity checks so the best displayed rate always favours booking direct.
  • Claim the reviews it already owns: 10,511 Google reviews is a remarkable trust asset — surface it ("rated by 10,000+ guests") alongside schema markup that lets Google display it in search results.
  • Publish the offers: festive and seasonal offers as real, linkable pages — currently the page exists but is empty during peak season.

7Proposed 90-day engagement

Month 1 — Fix & instrument Repair the storefront (new site, all pages live and fast), fix analytics (GTM + GA4 + Clarity), restore offers page, remove spam links, submit proper sitemap. First-ever funnel baseline.
Month 2 — Build equity Real venue photography on social, Reddit/Quora participation, review-request program on property, structured-data markup for search visibility, festive offer launch pages.
Month 3 — Prove it Delta report against this baseline: review score movement, booking-engine funnel conversion, direct-booking share, social growth. Roadmap for the next quarter.

8What this engagement needs to run

  • Access: hosting/website credentials (to repair the 500 error), Google Business Profile, booking engine settings, and social page admin rights.
  • Hotel-side inputs: festive and corporate offer inventory (for the offers pages), a room-assignment preference near event spaces, and selected guest photography permission.
  • Direction: target ambition (e.g., move TripAdvisor from 4.1 → 4.4 and rank into the top 5) to anchor monthly success criteria.
