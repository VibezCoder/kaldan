# 5. What I would specifically avoid

There are some tempting Awwwards-style techniques that I would not automatically introduce.

- Excessive horizontal scrolling: It can make a hospitality website frustrating to navigate.
- Constant parallax: It makes the experience visually noisy.
- Custom cursors everywhere: They can interfere with normal navigation.
- Too many pinned sections: They make the website feel artificially long.
- Animations on every text element: This destroys the impact of the important moments.
- Unnecessary 3D/WebGL: Only use it if it meaningfully contributes to the property's presentation.

Performance, mobile behaviour and reduced-motion accessibility must also be considered.

One research limitation: I could retrieve the live content and structure of all three websites, but the Awwwards elements pages and two of the live sites did not expose their complete interactive behaviour through the available browser inspection. Consequently, the interaction library above is my proposed adaptation, not a claim that every listed animation was verified on those exact sites. Mobbin's search connection also requires a paid Mobbin plan, so I could not retrieve additional reference sections there.
