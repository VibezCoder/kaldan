Answers to 36 guest questions about arrival, rooms, dining, activities and policies at Kaldan Samudhra Palace.

Your questions, considered

Good to know before you go.

Browse the palace’s published guest questions. Where property FAQs and group policies differ, we explain the uncertainty and ask you to confirm with the hotel rather than promise an unverified arrangement.

Where is Kaldan Samudhra Palace located?

Arrival & essentials

Kaldan Samudhra Palace is a luxury beachfront resort in Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu, offering a royal palace experience.

What is the address of Kaldan Samudhra?

Arrival & essentials

16/3B1B, East Coast Road, Devaneri, Chengalpattu, Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu - 603104.

What is the hotel telephone number?

Arrival & essentials

Hotel reception: +91 44 4568 1000↗. For reservations: +91 89 2581 4910↗.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Arrival & essentials

Check-in: 3:00 PM | Check-out: 12:00 PM

Is parking available at the hotel?

Arrival & essentials

Yes, complimentary valet parking is available for all the guests.

Does the hotel provide airport transfers?

Arrival & essentials

Yes, airport transfers can be arranged on request at an additional charge.

Can I bring a visitor to my room?

Arrival & essentials

The palace FAQ directs visitors to the lobby and restaurants and says they are not allowed in guest rooms. The group policy separately describes room visits at the hotel’s discretion until 9 PM. Please confirm the current palace visitor policy with the team before inviting anyone to your room.

Is the hotel suitable for honeymooners and families?

Arrival & essentials

Yes, the palace offers romantic getaways for couples and family-friendly activities.

Does the palace have private beach?

Arrival & essentials

Yes, the palace has a private beach deck and it can be accessed only by the residential guests.

Is your hotel pet-friendly?

Arrival & essentials

No, pets are not allowed in the premises.

Which airport is close to Kaldan Samudhra Palace?

Arrival & essentials

Chennai Airport is approximately 55 km from the hotel.

What’s the unique selling point of the palace?

Arrival & essentials

Royal Rajasthani Welcome: Experience a traditional Rajasthani welcome and enjoy a captivating folk performance. Palace Hi-Tea: Indulge in a luxurious hi-tea experience fit for royalty. Variety of Activities: Enjoy indoor and outdoor activities for kids and adults, ensuring a fun-filled stay. Certified Kids' Activities: Engage your children in certified activities that foster pride and encouragement. Signature Tiramisu: Savour our signature dessert, crafted to perfection. Unique Dining Experiences: Dine at our exclusive venues: Taj Deck, Gazzebo, and Beach Deck. Personalised Photo Souvenir: Receive a complimentary photoframe souvenir from our evening photoshoot. One of the Longest Pools: Relax in our expansive pool, one of the longest in the city.

What room categories are available at the palace?

Rooms & comfort

The resort offers Deluxe Room, Premium Room, Suites, and Luxury Villas.

Does the room has a locker to keep my valuables?

Rooms & comfort

Yes, all the rooms.

Do the rooms have private balconies with sea views?

Rooms & comfort

Yes, Luxury Villas offer stunning views of the Bay of Bengal.

This answer refers to the Luxury Villas, not to every accommodation category. Please confirm the view of your chosen room.

Is Wi-Fi available in all rooms?

Rooms & comfort

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available.

Are smoking rooms available?

Rooms & comfort

No, all rooms are non-smoking, but designated smoking areas are available.

Can I request an extra bed or baby cot?

Rooms & comfort

Yes, extra beds and baby cots are available on request (charges may apply).

Confirm permitted occupancy, your children’s ages and the applicable extra-bed or cot charge for your chosen category.

What dining options are available at the hotel?

Dining & drinks

The resort offers Dhani fine-dining restaurant which offers buffet and a la Carte. (Pre-booking Mandatory).

The dining collection also includes Somapuraa, Sagar Bar and Panch Mahal. Panch Mahal’s current operating status and hours should be reconfirmed.

Explore all dining settings↗
Can I request extra bedding (pillow, blanket, etc.)?

Dining & drinks

Please confirm this detail with our reservations team.

Does the hotel serve vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Dining & drinks

Yes, a variety of vegetarian, vegan, and Jain-friendly dishes are available.

Does the hotel have a bar?

Dining & drinks

Yes, the hotel offers a luxury bar with premium alcoholic beverages.

Do you allow outside liquor to be brought in?

Dining & drinks

Not in the bar or restaurant, as a residential guest you can bring and consume inside your room.

What is the Hi-Tea Timing?

Dining & drinks

Hi-Tea will be served from 5 PM till 6 PM.

Is there a candle light dinner available?

Dining & drinks

Yes, unique dining experience will be the perfect choice of candle light dinner and private family dinner for 6 to 8 persons (Taj Deck, Gazebo, and Beach Deck).

Does the resort have a swimming pool?

Experiences & events

Yes. The experience collection lists Sagar Pool with published hours of 7:00 AM–8:00 PM. Swimwear is mandatory; children must be supervised. Confirm current access and operating arrangements with the hotel.

Is there a spa or wellness centre?

Experiences & events

Yes, the hotel offers Ayurvedic and international spa treatments for relaxation.

What activities are available at the resort?

Experiences & events

The resort offers beach yoga, water sports, cultural performances, and guided tours of Mahabalipuram.

Activities, external tours and water-based experiences may need advance arrangement; confirm the current programme, provider, conditions and charges. Follow staff and coastal safety advice.

Browse the recreation catalogue↗
Does the hotel offer banquet and wedding venues?

Experiences & events

Yes, the palace is a luxury wedding destination, offering grand banquet halls and beachfront event spaces.

Can I host corporate events and conferences at the resort?

Experiences & events

Yes, Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers state-of-the-art conference rooms and meeting spaces for corporate gatherings.

What is the cancellation policy?

Booking & house rules

The cancellation policy depends on the type of booking. Guests are advised to check their booking confirmation or contact the hotel for details.

The published source includes different seven-day and 72-hour schedules, different no-show penalties and offer-specific rules. Obtain the cancellation conditions for your particular reservation in writing.

Read the policy reference↗
As a dining guest, can I use your swimming pool and beach access?

Booking & house rules

No, swimming pool facility and the access to the beach are limited for our residential guests.

A separately booked Day Use Package lists pool access; do not assume a dining booking includes it or that the package includes beach access. Confirm any package entitlement directly.

Does the hotel have Hookah lounge?

Booking & house rules

No, there is no Hookah Lounge and Hookah is not permitted within the premises. We advise guests not to carry by themselves.

Is wearing swimming costumes mandatory for the pool?

Booking & house rules

Yes, it is mandatory to wear a swimming costume.

Does the hotel allow guests to bring their own kitchen equipment?

Booking & house rules

No, the hotel does not allow guests to bring any kitchen equipment.

Does the hotel allow guests to bring their own DSLR or professional cameras?

Booking & house rules

The palace FAQ says professional cameras and DSLRs are not permitted. The group photography policy separately describes written approval for professional or commercial shoots. Please confirm the property-specific rule and obtain any required permission before bringing equipment.
