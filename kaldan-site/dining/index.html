At the palace table

Dhani — All-day Dining

An exquisite restaurant in Mahabalipuram, where flavours from around the globe come together with the charm of local delights. It offers both à la carte and buffet options, complete with live cooking counters where you can personalise your favourite dishes.

At the palace table

Somapuraa — Bar

From its exquisite selection of beverages—featuring fine wines, scotch, whisky, and brandy—to signature cocktails served daily, this bar provides the perfect retreat for those seeking relaxation and unwinding in the city.

Hours & details↗

At the palace table

Sagar Bar — Pool Bar

Overlooking a stunning 144-metre pool, this bar serves a tempting array of crafted drinks, perfectly paired with gourmet light bites. Relax on comfy lounge beds, surrounded by lush greenery, making it a favourite spot for all who visit us.

Hours & details↗

At the palace table

Panch Mahal

Behold! An ambrosial endeavor sewn together with the augustness of Indian heritage and the epic milieu of oceanic splendor. Embark on a gastronomical odyssey of epic proportions through five of India’s most fabled regal cuisines: the affluent plains of Punjab, the arid desert kingdoms of Rajasthan, the luxuriant Nawabi courts of Lucknow, the opulence of Hyderabad palaces, and the frigid poshness of Kashmir. The epitome of culinary excellence and impeccable hospitality. FIVE ROYAL CUISINES. ONE EXTRAORDINARY FEAST

Just for you

An occasion of your own.

The palace’s unique dining experiences offer intimate settings at the Taj Deck, Gazebo and Beach Deck. The published experience rate starts at ₹16,000++ for one occasion; the current menu, taxes, capacity and price must be reconfirmed.

The Royal Affair package asks for dinner reservations at least 24 hours ahead. Ask the team which advance notice applies to your chosen experience.

Good to know

Before you take your seat.

Dhani’s published FAQ asks guests to pre-book. Tell the dining team about allergies and dietary requirements in advance; the FAQ lists vegetarian, vegan and Jain-friendly dishes.

A dining reservation does not include access to the swimming pool or beach. Confirm any separately booked day-use package directly with the hotel.
