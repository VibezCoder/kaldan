Behold! An ambrosial endeavor sewn together with the augustness of Indian heritage and the epic milieu of oceanic splendor. Embark on a gastronomical odyssey of epic proportions through five of India’s most fabled regal cuisines: the affluent plains of Punjab, the arid desert kingdoms of Rajasthan, the luxuriant Nawabi courts of Lucknow, the opulence of Hyderabad palaces, and the frigid poshness of Kashmir. The epitome of culinary excellence and impeccable hospitality. FIVE ROYAL CUISINES. ONE EXTRAORDINARY FEAST

PANCH MAHAL - A Glorious Culinary Voyage by the Sea

Panch Mahal, the forthcoming jewel within our palace, is a poetic homage to India's royal kitchens, architectural grandeur, and the five elements of life—air, water, fire, earth, and space. Framed by Mahabalipuram's waves, it features gazebos that rise with the elegance of India's cultural legacy, floating serenely above still waters. As you settle in, surrounded by your loved ones, the gazebos glow under the soft embrace of candlelight, cast by majestic chandeliers. The night sky, adorned with stars, can be seen through the glass roof, adding a celestial touch to your meal. Meanwhile, the stage plays host to live classical music and cultural showcases, enriching the royal ambience. We are the best among restaurants in Mahabalipuram due to our cuisine. We draw inspiration from the royal kitchens of India's five princely states, each known for its distinct, royal fare:

Awadhi from Lucknow: Slow-cooked kebabs and aromatic biryanis

Punjabi: Rich gravies and tandoor specialities

Hyderabadi: Regal biryanis and spice-laden curries

Rajasthani: Bold, earthy flavours steeped in warrior heritage

Kashmiri: Saffron-infused dishes, dry fruits, and soul-soothing gravies

Every evening, you will be served royal thalis—a curated 7 to 10-course journey that transports you through the palaces and flavours of India's glorious past. Come, experience Panch Mahal, where luxury meets legacy, opulence mingles with nature, and you dine like royalty beneath the stars, by the sea.
