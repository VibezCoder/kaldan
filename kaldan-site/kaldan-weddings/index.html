Royal Darbara

With soaring 13.8 ft ceilings, sparkling chandeliers, and elegant floral-patterned carpets, this grand ballroom hall is the city’s premier venue for magnificent celebrations. With a capacity to host up to 1,000 guests, the space offers seamless customisation to bring your vision to life. It boasts a pillarless design, a spacious 2,564 sq. ft. pre-function area, and can be transformed into three smaller halls—Mewar, Marwar, and Shekhawati—each accommodating 300 guests, perfect for intimate gatherings. Be it a pre-wedding function, a post-wedding soirée, or a large-scale conference, our hall sets the stage for unforgettable experiences.

Published venue specifications

Sundarabagh

The epitome of outdoor event venues in Mahabalipuram, our lush Sundarabagh Lawn offers an expansive 55,000 sq. ft. of beautifully landscaped space by the beach. Enter through grand elephant statues, surrounded by swaying coconut trees and glowing warm lights, setting the perfect tone for your celebration. Whether you’re exchanging rings by the waves or sealing deals with the sound of the shore, this space is nothing short of magical. As the Best 5 Star Wedding Venue in ECR, it is ideal for large weddings, events, and celebrations, accommodating up to 3,000 guests and making every moment unforgettable! An expansive ocean-facing lawn, ideal for grand gatherings and evening weddings, sets the perfect stage for your pheras under a canopy of stars. With the sea as your backdrop and the towering palace in full view, this venue transforms your royal wedding dreams into reality.
