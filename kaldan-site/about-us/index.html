A royal spirit. A coastal soul.

Discover the story, Rajasthani architecture and heartfelt hospitality of Kaldan Samudhra Palace in Mahabalipuram.

Our story

Born of craft. Built for connection.

Spread across a 10-acre sparkling estate, our palace stands as a beacon of elegance, grandeur, and timeless beauty. It is revered as the ‘Immortal Palace’ for its seamless blend of three timeless cultures that make up the 'Golden Triangle'—the essence of nobility with a stunning replica of the Taj Mahal, the regal charm of Rajasthani architecture in the palace, and the tranquil allure of the nearby sea, reminiscent of Goa’s golden shores. From lavish accommodations to exquisite dining and vibrant cultural experiences, every moment here echoes these rich influences, ensuring a true connection to our royal heritage and a promise of an unforgettable experience.

Our Story: Founded by Dr S Jagathrakshakan, our palace was born from the fusion of Rajasthani architecture and palatial arts. Construction began in 2017 and was brought to life on December 9, 2021, through the skilled hands of artisans, sculptors, and builders from across India.

The people & the group

The vision behind the palace

Learn about the leadership and the wider Accord Group, as described in the hotel’s published story.

Dr. S. Jagathrakshakan - Chairman

Born in Vazhudhavur, Tamil Nadu, he comes from the Vanniyar community and has risen from modest beginnings to become a distinguished figure in politics, business, literature, and philanthropy. As Chairman of the Accord Group, his strategic vision and business expertise have been key drivers of the Group’s growth. A devoted philanthropist, he provides free healthcare to over 6,000 patients daily, facilitates 5,000 marriages every year, and cares for 5,000 adopted children. His village adoption initiative covers 150 villages, and he has been instrumental in renovating 5,500 temples. In his political career, he served as a Member of Parliament across three Lok Sabhas and held important ministerial positions in Information & Broadcasting, Renewable Energy, and Commerce & Industry. A scholar and author of 60 books, he also founded the Azhvargal Research Centre and Alwargal Aiyvu Mayam to promote Vaishnavism. His life stands as a testament to resilience, service, and a deep commitment to cultural preservation.

Dr. J. Srinisha - Managing Director

She holds a Master’s degree in Dental Surgery and plays a key role in driving the Accord Group’s medical and dental initiatives. As Director of the Accord Group, she was pivotal in setting up state-of-the-art, multi-disciplinary super-speciality hospitals in Chennai, including one of the region’s leading liver transplant centres. With a clear vision to expand accessible, high-quality healthcare across South India, she continues to spearhead the Group’s efforts in building a robust network of advanced hospitals. Dr. Srinisha also serves as a respected member of the Dental Council of India, contributing to the accreditation and evaluation of dental education institutions. Beyond healthcare, she is a Trustee of the Sri Lakshmi Ammal Educational Trust, furthering the Group’s commitment to education and social development.

Dr. J. Sundeep Anand - Managing Director

He is a dynamic professional with a postgraduate degree in Computer Science from Minnesota, USA, a Doctorate in Computer Science from the College of Engineering, Chennai, and additional qualifications in Management and Law. As the Managing Director of the Accord Group, he has been central to the Group’s evolution into a diverse and multi-dimensional business conglomerate. He also serves as the Managing Trustee of the Sri Lakshmi Ammal Educational Trust, playing a key role in the growth and development of Bharath University and the Group’s broader educational ventures. Dr. Anand oversees the Group’s daily operations, consistently driving its expansion and ensuring long-term success.

Education

Under the Sri Lakshmi Ammal Educational Trust, the Accord Group’s education arm—Bharath Institute of Higher Education and Research (BIHER)—has grown into a premier multi-stream university with over 10,000 students and six expansive campuses across Tamil Nadu and Puducherry. Its institutions include top-ranking medical, dental, engineering, nursing, physiotherapy, and law colleges like Sree Balaji Medical College & Hospital, JR Medical College, and SLIMS. Recognised as the ‘Best Multi-Stream Private University in India’ by ASSOCHAM, BIHER is known for quality education, research, and a commitment to societal upliftment.

Hospitality

The Accord Hotels & Resorts and Kaldan Samudhra Palace bring together luxury, authentic South Indian hospitality, and world-class service across key destinations like Chennai, Ooty, Mahabalipuram, and Pondicherry. With a focus on personalised guest experiences and initiatives like the JUJU Guest Experience Ambassador program, the Group ensures every stay is memorable, warm, and indulgent.

Health Care

The Accord Group’s healthcare venture, Rela Hospital in Chennai, operated by JR Super Speciality Hospitals Pvt. Ltd., is a leading quaternary care centre founded in 2017 under the guidance of Dr. S. Jagathrakshakan and Prof. Mohamed Rela. Home to one of the world’s largest liver ICUs, the 450-bed facility is renowned for cutting-edge technology, global clinical expertise, and compassionate patient care.

Pharmaceutical Industry

Through NJK Pharma and Teyro Labs, the Accord Group is making a strong mark in pharmaceuticals, focusing on affordable, high-quality medicines and innovation. NJK Pharma is building a cGMP-compliant facility for WHO-standard formulations, while Teyro Labs specialises in oncology and complex drug manufacturing with USFDA and EUGMP-approved facilities, offering global R&D, CRO, and CMO services.

Brewery

Accord Distillers and Brewers operates a state-of-the-art 300 HL Automatic Brewery Unit with Meura and Ziemann technology, producing up to 1 million hectolitres annually, expandable to 1.5 million. With exclusive Carlsberg tie-ups, IMFL manufacturing, and a growing export footprint, the ₹500-crore unit is a major player in Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Minerals – Panyam Cement

Established in 1955, Panyam Cement became a household name for strength and quality, fueling major Indian infrastructure projects. After a revival led by Dr. Srinisha Jagathrakshakan and partners, Panyam is back in action, serving South India with renewed vigour.

Mining of Rare Earth

With deep expertise in beach mineral extraction, the Group specialises in ilmenite, rutile, and zircon, crucial for both everyday products and high-tech industries. Backed by international ties, they are gearing up to produce five to ten rare earth chemicals.

Power Generation

The Group powers forward with 300 MW of coal-based energy near Chennai, a 660x2 MW plant near Tuticorin, and a 1320 MW gas plant in Andhra Pradesh. Adding to the green grid, they’re setting up a 100 MW solar plant in Tamil Nadu.

Information Technology

The Group’s IT division operates two specialised companies. One delivers web-enabled ERP solutions, web hosting, and legacy system management for European clients. The other focuses on mobility solutions, big data analytics, and next-generation technologies. Together, they build scalable platforms, enhance digital ecosystems, and provide customised software development services to help businesses stay future-ready.

Engineering – CECL and Chiprime

From iconic landmarks to cutting-edge tech, CECL and Chiprime drive the Group’s engineering excellence. CECL, with its rich legacy since 1947, shapes India's skyline, while Chiprime leads in semiconductor innovation, gearing up for India's own fab facility.

Sports – Jagath Cricket Academy (JCA)

Jagath Cricket Academy (JCA) in Pondicherry, a BCCI-approved facility, is dedicated to nurturing young cricket talent. With expert coaching in batting, bowling, and fielding, JCA prepares players for competitive cricket. The academy offers top-notch training grounds and fitness routines, helping players achieve peak performance.

Media – Accord Voice

Accord Voice is a prominent Tamil-language news platform under the Accord Group. Covering topics from sports and entertainment to politics and global events, it offers timely updates and in-depth analysis. With a focus on keeping its readers informed, Accord Voice continues to shape public discourse and provide valuable insights into current affairs.
