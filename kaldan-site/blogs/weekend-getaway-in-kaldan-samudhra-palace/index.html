Weekend getaway in Kaldan Samudhra Palace

From the hotel’s published journal

Kaldan Samudhra Palace is one of the most luxurious resorts available in Chennai. Its intricately designed interiors take you back to those times of royalty, making you feel truly royal. Kaldan Samudhra Palace is one such resort that has something for everyone, regardless of age, whether it's a child who wants to spend their weekend playing on the beachside or a couple wanting to spend their weekend privately, or it's just a family outing; Kaldan has you covered.

If you are looking to relax, go on an adventure, or hit a cultural retreat, a weekend at Kaldan Samudhra Palace promises to be all of these and more. Inspired by the majestic palaces of Rajasthan, its meticulously carved arches, grand domes, and lavish courtyards transport you to the era of Indian royalty as soon as you step in.

Luxurious Accommodations

From elegantly designed rooms to spacious suites that exude royal charm, Kaldan offers a variety of accommodations. The rooms are well equipped with modern amenities like high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, caller ID phones, in-room electronic safes, and much more. The Villas also come with balconies, providing you with a private space with a stunning view of the ocean.

Unwinding at the spa and pool

Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers an amazing spa & pool, helping you rejuvenate your body and mind. The spa at Kaldan Samudhra offers soothing Ayurvedic massages, herbal therapies & refreshing facials designed to melt away your stress. The stunning Sagar pool offers you the opportunity to simply lounge with a drink in hand, soaking in the breathtaking coastal views or take a kayak ride in the longest pool of the city.

Exploring Mahabalipuram's Cultural Heritage

Though Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers enough to keep you entertained within its premises, there are a lot of stunning cultural heritage sites in close proximity that are worth exploring. Mahabalipuram, located just a few km away from Kaldan, is a UNESCO World Heritage site, & it is known for its ancient rock-cut temples & sculptures. Key attractions include the Shore Temple, which is considered an architectural marvel dating back to the 8th century, standing against the backdrop of the Bay of Bengal. Panch Rathas & Arjuna's penance are a few other cultural sites that have a rich, interesting story, making it the perfect spot to explore the culture and tradition.

Exquisite Dining

Kaldan Samudhra Palace dining is not just about a meal; it's an experience of flavours, heritage, and luxury. Dhani, Kaldan's signature restaurant, offers exceptional dining, while taking a sip of spirits is on the menu at Somapuraa. From indulgent royal feasts to fresh coastal delights, Kaldan offers it all.

Kaldan also has an evening exclusive space called the 'Chill With Grills', where you get live music, delicious barbeques and refreshing beverages with the ocean just by your side.

Evening Entertainment and Cultural Performances

Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers a variety of cultural performances that celebrate India's rich traditions. Rajasthani folk music, Kathak performances are a few of the live shows that guests can enjoy. Kaldan offers interactive sessions for guests where they can try traditional dance moves.

Couple's private getaway

Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers intimate beachside candlelight dinners for couples at the Gazebo. Couple spa retreats, sunset strolls, beach picnics, and poolside dining under the miniature Taj Mahal are a few things that couples can explore. Kaldan Samudhra Palace also offers kite flying & other beach games for couples looking for shore-side fun.

Kids Fun Zone

Kaldan Samudhra Palace offers an exclusive kids' play area with slides, swings, and interactive activities like foosball, chess, table tennis, and so much more. The traditional Rajasthani puppet shows and folk tales, arts & crafts workshops, cooking classes & mini treasure hunts are a few of the other things offered by Kaldan Samudhra Palace to keep the little ones engaged.

Kaldan Samudhra Palace is your one-stop destination for a relaxing and luxurious weekend, where you won't forget the warm and royal welcoming experience you had.
