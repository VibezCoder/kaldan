10 Exciting Activities To Plan Around Mahabalipuram Resorts

The Indian Eastern Coasts, towards the peninsular tip, lies the coastal town of Mahabalipuram, also known as Mamallapuram and is renowned for its rich history, stunning architecture, and beautiful beaches. As a popular travel destination, Mahabalipuram offers a myriad of activities that attract tourists from all over the world. Whether you are staying at one of the luxurious resorts or a cosy guesthouse, there is no shortage of exciting things to do. Here are ten activities that you should plan around the Mahabalipuram resorts to make the most of your visit.

Explore the Shore Temple

The Shore Temple is recognised by UNESCO as a World Heritage site and is one of the oldest stone temples in South India. A testament to the Dravidian culture, this temple was built in the 8th century during the reign of the Pallava dynasty. The temple is perched on the shores of the Bay of Bengal, offering a spectacular view of the ocean. Visiting the Shore Temple is like stepping back in time, and it’s a must-see for history enthusiasts and architecture lovers.

Visit the Pancha Rathas

The Pancha Rathas or the Five Rathas, are monolithic rock-cut structures that resemble chariots. Each ratha is intricately carved and named after the Pandavas from the Mahabharata. The UNESCO heritage showcase the architectural prowess of the Pallavas and provide insight into the historical and cultural significance of the era. Walking among these magnificent monuments is a surreal experience.

Discover Arjuna’s Penance

Arjuna’s Penance as it is more popularly known as, is a massive rock relief carved into the face of two adjacent boulders. It is also called The Descent of the Ganges. You can find intricate carving that depict various scenes from Hindu mythology, including the descent of the Ganges River to Earth and the penance of Arjuna. The sheer size and detail of this sculpture make it one of the most remarkable attractions in Mahabalipuram.

Relax on Mahabalipuram Beach

Mahabalipuram Beach provides a tranquil escape with its expanse of golden sands. It’s an ideal destination for those looking to escape the city’s chaos. Whether you wish to bask in the sun, enjoy a gentle walk, or participate in beach activities, this beach caters to all preferences. The calming sound of the waves and the pleasant sea breeze make the atmosphere all the more relaxing.

Surfing Lessons

For adventure seekers, Mahabalipuram offers excellent surfing opportunities. Several surfing schools provide lessons for beginners and advanced surfers. The waves along the coastline are perfect for surfing, and the warm water makes it an enjoyable experience year-round. Learning to surf in Mahabalipuram is not only thrilling but also a great way to connect with nature.

Visit the Crocodile Bank

The Madras Crocodile Bank Trust is home to a variety of crocodiles, alligators, and other reptiles. Founded by conservationist Romulus Whitaker, this centre promotes the conservation of amphibians and reptiles. Visitors can learn about their habits and habitats. It’s an educational and exciting activity for both adults and children.

Explore the Seashell Museum

The India Seashell Museum in Mahabalipuram is one of the largest seashell museums in Asia and showcases some of the most rare seashells from around the world. The museum also features exhibits on marine life and the history of shells. For those interested in marine biology or simply looking for an unusual attraction, the Seashell Museum is a must-visit.

Boating on Muttukadu Lake

Muttukadu Lake, located a short drive from Mahabalipuram, is a beautiful spot for boating and water sports. You have multiple activities in the lake including pedal boats, row boats, and motor boats. The breathtaking beauty of the lake makes for a peaceful and enjoyable outing. Water sports enthusiasts can also try windsurfing and kayaking.

Take a Cycling Tour

Cycling is a fantastic way to explore Mahabalipuram and its surroundings. Several guided cycling tours take visitors through scenic routes, historical landmarks, and local villages. Cycling allows you to experience the town at a pace that lets you appreciate the natural beauty and cultural heritage of the area. It’s an eco-friendly and healthy activity that can be enjoyed by people of all ages.

Enjoy Local Cuisine

There are numerous restaurants and eateries offering delicious dishes and Mahabalipuram is known for the best. From fresh fish curry to prawn masala, the flavours of Mahabalipuram are sure to tantalize your taste buds. Enjoying a meal at a beachfront restaurant while watching the sunset is a memorable experience.

Accommodation Options

Mahabalipuram offers a wide range of accommodation options, from luxurious resorts to budget-friendly guesthouses. Many resorts provide stunning views of the ocean, top-notch amenities, and easy access to major attractions. Whether you’re looking for a relaxing retreat or an adventure-filled stay, you’ll find the perfect place to rest and rejuvenate.

Among the top accommodations in Mahabalipuram is Kaldan Samudhra, a premier resort that offers a blend of luxury and tradition. With its magnificent architecture, world-class facilities, and breathtaking views of the Bay of Bengal, Kaldan Samudhra provides an unforgettable experience. The resort’s well-appointed rooms, exquisite dining options, and impeccable service ensure a comfortable and lavish stay. It’s an ideal spot for both leisure and business travellers, making it a standout choice in the heart of Mahabalipuram.

Mahabalipuram is a treasure trove of exciting activities and attractions that caters to people of all ages. Whether you are interested in history, adventure, relaxation, or culinary delights, this coastal town has something to offer. So, plan your visit to Mahabalipuram and explore these ten exciting activities for an unforgettable experience. Ready to make some memories in Mahabalipuram? Start planning your trip today!
