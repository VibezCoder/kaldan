Best Destination Beach Weddings

From the hotel’s published journal

Journal features may refer to past events, seasonal packages or earlier service information. Confirm current facilities, rates and arrangements directly with the hotel.

Weddings are one of the most magical moments of our lives, making that special day even more memorable with a Destination Beach Wedding. The Kaldan Samudhra, rated as one of the best Resorts in Mahabalipuram, is the perfect wedding venue by the seaside for you to plan your fairytale wedding.

The 5-star resort by ECR Chennai offers an array of unique indoor and Outdoor Wedding Venues within the palace, including a magnificent seaside lawn beside the Bay of Bengal. The sprawling 10-acre venue can accommodate as many as 1000+ guests. The palatial resort has over 106 rooms, from luxurious pool-facing villas to superior suites equipped with all modern facilities to ensure a comfortable stay.

Treat your wedding guests with the royal experience as traditional Rajasthani folk artists who give you a majestic greeting with Nagara, bigul & heart-warming arati. The opulent architecture inspired by Rajaputani & Mughal culture, the whole premises takes you back in time to the glorious royal lifestyle. The legendary sun rise by the east coast is breathtaking and Instagram worthy & photoshoots by the sea are magical.

Our award-winning chefs would personalise the menu for your wedding celebrations, from authentic Mughlai cuisine to international dishes, your choices are endless. The Kaldan Samudhra has beautiful spots for pre-wedding shoots, its green lush garden, grandiose pool, gazebos and mini Taj Mahal- a grand symbol of love and sea view balcony are any photographer’s dream come true.

The best time to plan your wedding in Goa is between mid-November to mid-March, when the sunshine is just about enough to give you a warm feeling accompanied by a cool sea breeze.

Get in touch with us to plan your Royal Wedding at the Kaldan Samudhra: reservations.samudhra@kaldanhotels.com
