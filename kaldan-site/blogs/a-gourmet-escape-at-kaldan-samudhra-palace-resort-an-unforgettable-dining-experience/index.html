A Gourmet Escape at Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort: An Unforgettable Dining Experience

At Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort, guests are invited to embark on a culinary journey like no other, all set against the breathtaking coastal scenery.…

From the hotel’s published journal

Journal features may refer to past events, seasonal packages or earlier service information. Confirm current facilities, rates and arrangements directly with the hotel.

At Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort, guests are invited to embark on a culinary journey like no other, all set against the breathtaking coastal scenery. Whether you’re lounging by the beach with grilled delicacies, dining in a regal setting with world-class cuisine, or enjoying a refreshing cocktail, Kaldan Samudhra Palace ensures a truly unforgettable dining experience.

Dhani: A Royal Dining Affair

For those looking for a refined culinary experience, Dhani, the resort’s fine dining restaurant, is the perfect choice. Offering a range of global cuisines, Dhani combines bold flavours with premium ingredients to create extraordinary dishes. From traditional Indian fare to international gourmet options, the diverse menu caters to every palate. On Sundays, Dhani offers an indulgent brunch from 1 pm to 3:30 pm, making weekends even more special.

What sets Dhani apart is its distinctive royal atmosphere. Adorned with luxurious décor, intricate artwork, and soft lighting, the restaurant exudes elegance and sophistication. Each meal feels like a special occasion, making it ideal for intimate dinners, family gatherings, or business events. The regal charm, paired with carefully crafted dishes, ensures an unforgettable dining experience.

Somapuraa: Unwind and Reconnect in Style

After a day of activities or an exquisite meal, head to Somapuraa, the resort’s chic lounge bar. Whether you’re catching up with friends or enjoying family time, Somapuraa provides a stylish yet cosy setting to relax. With a handpicked selection of spirits, wines, and signature cocktails, it’s the ideal spot to wind down after a day of exploration.

The lounge’s intimate atmosphere, complete with plush seating and ambient lighting, creates the perfect backdrop for unwinding. Whether you’re celebrating a milestone or savouring a quiet evening, Somapuraa offers a welcoming environment to reconnect over drinks.

A Unique Culinary Experience

Dining at Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort in Mahabalipuram is more than just a meal—it’s an experience that engages all the senses. Whether you’re enjoying live grills on the beach, savouring gourmet dishes in a royal setting, or sipping cocktails in a chic lounge, each venue offers something unique. Every meal is an opportunity to relish exquisite flavours, stunning views, and your loving company at this incredible Palace.
