How to Choose the Perfect Banquet Hall for Your Next Event?

When a big event is near the sight, you don't just plan it; you dream of it! From every little detail to big decoration, you would have planned & watched…

From the hotel’s published journal

When a big event is near the sight, you don't just plan it; you dream of it! From every little detail to big decoration, you would have planned & watched it happen in your dream. All that is left is to find a place and a bunch of experts to execute it perfectly, the way you dreamt of Kaldan Samudhra Palace is here to make your dream come true.

Here we've listed some essential tips to help you choose the right venue.

1. Define Your Event's Requirements

Before looking for the venue, list down your requirements for the event. Things like the type of event (corporate, wedding or social), the number of guests and any specific needs for layout (cluster or theatre set-up) and AV setups can be considered. Figuring out your expectations in advance will help you filter out options that better align with your needs.

2. Assess the Ambience

Everyone is a prince/princess on their big day and wants to be treated that way. The ambience of the banquet hall and interior work play a major role in creating a royally appealing atmosphere. If you're looking for a royal setting with the ambience of the palace, a luxurious, aesthetic vibe with chandeliers and intricate designs, Kaldan Samudhra Palace's 'Royal Durbara' offers grandeur inspired by Rajasthani architecture and heritage. With towering ceilings and an expansive layout, Royal Durbara provides a regal backdrop that's perfect for large weddings, galas or formal corporate gatherings. The Royal Durbara can be split into three exquisite halls: Marwar, Mewar & Shekawath. Where many events can happen simultaneously if your requirement of the space is not as large. If your event demands a more intimate yet elegant space, the palace's newest addition, "Diwan", the ballroom, might be ideal. Combining luxury with a touch of royalty and modern amenities, this refined venue transforms corporate venues or social gatherings into unforgettable experiences. A gala dinner or an executive seminar, Diwan’s warm & elegant design will enhance the exclusivity of your event.

3. Consider Location and Accessibility

Accessibility is an essential aspect to be considered, especially when it comes to large events where attendees may be travelling from different areas. To ensure that the guests can arrive with ease, choose a banquet hall with a convenient location and ample parking or transport options.

The banquet halls at Kaldan Samudhra Palace are not only scenic but also well-connected, allowing easy access for both local and out-of-town guests.

4. Explore Outdoor Options for Unique Experiences

The Sundarabagh lawn at Kaldan Samudhra Palace is ideal for a mesmerising outdoor wedding event. In the open space under the sky with a stunning beach view, this beachfront sprawling lawn is all you need for ideal beach weddings, cocktail parties or any other outdoor celebrations. Nothing can beat the beauty of nature and the amount of charm it adds to your event. The natural lighting, breeze of air, sight of the ocean, and the free-flowing guests will give an exceptional natural glow to the event

5. Verify Facilities and Services

The needs and expectations of every event differ, and it's crucial to make a list of things you need for your event. AV equipment, lighting options, catering services, accessibility, and accommodations are some, to name a few. The banquet halls at Kaldan Samudhra Palace are equipped with the latest amenities and modern facilities, ensuring that your event runs smoothly from start to end. The exceptional hospitality & service by our staff will make your experience better.

6. Check Availability and Plan Ahead

Everything should be planned ahead, and when it comes to events, planning should be given utmost importance. Finalising your venue and checking whether they offer all the amenities you need are some of the major aspects of planning your event. The Palace's venues, including the latest ballroom 'Diwan', offer a selection of unique and versatile spaces that can be tailored to your event requirements. Booking ahead ensures you secure the perfect venue and have time to make any customisations.

7. Customise to Reflect Your Theme

At Kaldan Samudhra Palace, whatever the event is, right from decorations to the food, everything could be customised based on the needs of the client. Each venue has its personality, from the grand historical feel of Royal Durbara to the serene, open atmosphere of Sundarabagh, providing plenty of options to fit the theme you have chosen for your big day. Choosing the best place to host your event will halve your worries.

Staff at Kaldan Samudhra Palace, with their exceptional "service from the heart", will make your stay there seamless and luxurious, making you feel like true royalty. With versatile spaces at Kaldan Samudhra Palace, like Royal Durbara, Diwan and Sundarabhaag, you will find the ideal venue for any occasion, turning your next event into a beautiful celebration.
