The palace journal · Culture & coast

Welcome Rituals at our Luxury Palace by the Sea

The grand royal welcome – traditional dances, mesmerising display of Rajasthani architecture or perhaps the delicious Rajasthani cuisine sounds…

From the hotel’s published journal

Journal features may refer to past events, seasonal packages or earlier service information. Confirm current facilities, rates and arrangements directly with the hotel.

The grand royal welcome – traditional dances, mesmerising display of Rajasthani architecture or perhaps the delicious Rajasthani cuisine sounds enchanting, right?

Tucked amidst the caress of ocean waves and whispering green scapes, Kaldan is known for its luxurious ambience and tranquil amenities. Rajasthan, renowned for its rich culture, heritage, captivating safaris, sprawling dunes, ancient mountain ranges, and diverse wildlife, stands unparalleled as a destination. Our commitment lies in extending a regal welcome, allowing the guests to savour the essence of royal Rajasthan exquisitely. The very first Rajasthani folk performance was in the early seventeenth century. At Kaldan, the royalty is carried throughout with opulent decor, delicious Rajasthani cuisine, and even traditional Rajasthani dancers performing for our guests.

In Rajasthan, the phrase “Atithi Devo Bhava” equates a guest with divinity, emphasising that welcoming a guest parallels welcoming and honouring God. Upon arrival at the Palace, guests are greeted with the vibrant “Kachichhi Godi” dance with Rajasthani Nagada drums —a cherished Rajasthani tradition. Adorned in traditional Rajasthani attire, women joyously shower flower petals while musicians at the entrance serenade with melodious tunes from classical Rajasthani instruments, creating a heartwarming reception. Guests are offered to adorn the traditional Rajasthani colourful turbans that can be taken home as a souvenir of the immortal experiences at the Palace.

Guests are warmly welcomed with a tika, a vibrant mark of vermilion applied to their foreheads, followed by the traditional Arti ceremony. Arti, a sacred ritual, involves seeking blessings from the divine by lighting ghee-filled lamps arranged in a small tray. As the lamps flicker to life, the tray is gently spun in a circular motion before the honoured guest, symbolizing a gesture of heartfelt welcome.

A royal Rajasthani welcome at Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort is an experience filled with grandeur and traditional hospitality. The palace is adorned with colourful fabrics and traditional elements to create a regal atmosphere. The royal treatments continue as you’re served with Rajasthani welcome drinks, live performances featuring folk music, the soulful tunes of the sarangi, and rhythmic beats of the dhol set are all for an experience that transcends mere hospitality and ventures into the realm of enchantment. The experience of a royal Rajasthani welcome at Kaldan Samudhra Palace Resort is not merely a lavish affair; it is a solemn homage to the cultural legacy of the majestic land of Rajasthan. It leaves an indelible impression, weaving together history, art, and hospitality into an unforgettable tapestry of memories.

The crescendo of the evening culminates with a display of Rajasthani Folk performances. The rhythm of drums reverberates through the air, signalling the dancers adorned in vibrant costumes move with graceful precision. Amidst the melodic tunes, the courtyard transforms into a spectacle of colours and movements.

At Kaldan Samudhra Palace Beach Resort, we pride ourselves on creating an enchanting blend of Rajasthan’s regal traditions on the serene allure of the coastline. Join us in this cultural extravaganza, where every space is a testament to the timeless charm of Rajasthan’s heritage, inviting you to embark on a journey filled with splendour and tranquillity.

For those seeking respite and a touch of magic, Kaldan Samudhra Palace Gates are open, ushering in an era of unparalleled hospitality and unforgettable memories.
