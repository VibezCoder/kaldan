The palace journal · Celebrations

Proposals, weddings, anniversaries, parties – our banquet

When it comes to planning a memorable event, the venue is often the deciding factor that can make or break the experience. From intimate proposals to grand weddings and anniversary celebrations, the venue sets the stage for the entire affair. The right venue can create an atmosphere of sophistication, glamour, romance or whimsy, depending on the theme and style of the event. Whether it’s a historic mansion, a chic city rooftop, or a picturesque beachfront, the venue is the canvas upon which the event is painted. It is the backdrop for the memories that will be created, the conversations that will be had, and the emotions that will be shared. A well-chosen venue can enhance the decor, amplify the mood, and elevate the guest experience. From the lighting to the layout, every detail of the venue contributes to the overall ambience and impact of the event. With so much at stake, choosing the right venue is an essential element of successful event planning and one that requires careful consideration and attention to detail.

Kaldan offers an exceptional range of venues that cater to all types of events and celebrations. Whether you’re looking for a Grand Banquet Hall, a lush lawn, or a stunning beachside, the resort has it all. Our banquet hall with a seating capacity of 800 is the epitome of luxury, featuring elegant decor and state-of-the-art facilities that can accommodate large groups of guests. The lawn with a seating capacity of 1500 is a serene outdoor space that offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape, making it the perfect venue for a romantic wedding or a family picnic. For those who love to party or are looking for an ideal spot to propose their one and only, the Taj Deck is an ideal venue, with its vibrant ambience and a miniature Taj Mahal as a backdrop. What sets Kaldan apart is the attention to detail and personalised service provided by their staff. From the decor to the food and beverages, everything is carefully crafted to meet the unique needs and preferences of each guest. With such an incredible range of venues to choose from, Kaldan is the perfect destination to celebrate in style and create unforgettable memories that will last a lifetime.

Kaldan, a Luxury Beachside Resort Near Chennai, offers the perfect romantic setting for couples to pop the question and make their proposal a truly unforgettable experience. With breathtaking views of the ocean and pristine sandy beaches, the resort provides a picturesque backdrop that can add to the magic of the moment. The serene and tranquil atmosphere of the resort is ideal for setting the tone for a romantic evening, complete with candlelit dinners, champagne toast, and a gentle sea breeze. The resorts also offer a range of special packages that can be customised to meet the couple’s specific needs, including private beachside dinners, rose-petal strewn rooms, and a host of other romantic surprises. The friendly and attentive staff at the resorts can help create the perfect ambience and provide discreet and professional assistance in planning the perfect proposal. With so much on offer, it is no wonder that Kaldan is fast becoming Chennai’s favourite luxury beachside resort as the perfect destination for couples to make their romantic proposal.

Our Rajasthani-themed beachside resort offers a magical poolside setting that evokes the vibrant culture and rich heritage of Rajasthan. It is an ideal spot for couples to propose and celebrate pre-wedding festivities, such as engagement parties, cocktail receptions, and mehndi ceremonies. With traditional architecture and lush greenery, the poolside provides stunning backdrops for memorable moments and pictures. The festive atmosphere is perfect for dancing, mingling, and enjoying delicious food and drinks.

Choosing between the Sundarabagh Lawn and Royal Durbara for the main wedding event or grand celebrations can leave you in a dilemma. Both of these spectacular venues are sure to impress and leave a lasting impression. The Sundarabagh Lawn is a lush outdoor space that offers breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape and can accommodate a large number of guests. On the other hand, the Royal Durbara is an opulent indoor venue that exudes elegance and grandeur, making it perfect for more intimate gatherings.

At our resort, every event is a unique and memorable experience, thanks to the dedicated team of professionals who work tirelessly to ensure that your event is a resounding success. Whether it is a grand wedding celebration at the opulent banquet hall or an intimate ceremony on the plush lawn, the team will leave no stone unturned to make your event unforgettable. With a staff that boasts of members with years of experience in event planning and management, the team has the expertise to handle all aspects of your event, from decor and lighting to catering and hospitality. They work closely with you to understand your preferences and requirements and create a customized plan that reflects your vision and style.
