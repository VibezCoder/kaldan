The Journey of Kaldan Samudhra: A Look Back at the First Year

From the hotel’s published journal

Kaldan, which means an immortal place in Paradise, is your modern-day palace of dreams that offers more than just a place to stay. Here, you can enjoy a range of luxurious amenities and experiences that will make your holiday truly unforgettable. The resort boasts 106 rooms, suites, and villas, as well as the longest swimming pool (in Tamil Nadu), sprawling lawns, and a private beach.

You can expect to be pampered like royalty while enjoying curated experiences such as spa treatments, yoga classes, and meditation sessions. For your dining pleasure, Kaldan offers a global selection of cuisines, ranging from traditional Indian specialities to international delicacies.

Kaldan also offers plenty of activities that will help you make the most of your holiday. Whether you are looking for some beach fun or are more interested in exploring the area’s local culture, Kaldan has something for everyone. At Kaldan, you can enjoy the best of both worlds – a luxurious stay with all the modern amenities, as well as the opportunity to explore the local area and experience the culture and traditions of Tamil Nadu.

Kaldan is the perfect place to nurture the royalty residing in every heart and aims to pamper that prince or princess within you with its luxurious amenities and experiences.

Despite the resort’s proximity to other upscale Beach View Resorts, Kaldan Samudhra has piqued the interests of many tourists and wedding planners. Over the past year, many guests booked their stay, and we are happy to share that they have added to our ever-growing count of highly satisfied customers. Their positive feedback has strengthened our belief in our motto “Service from the Heart” and we are geared up to go above and beyond expectations.

“Had the privilege of visiting this property for a relative’s marriage, and my oh my this property completely blew off my mind. The location is picture perfect, the architecture is royal, the services are top class and the experience was amazing. Generally, food in such 5-star properties is pretty average, but the team here has managed to set that right! Food quality and variety of options was the highlight. I am truly glad, that I got an opportunity to spend the best 24 hours of my life” – Kartik Iyer

“The property took great care of us. The buffet spread was lovely and we had a great hi tea time with the in-house entertainment. The dip in the pool was quite refreshing. Thank u team and keep up the great service. Definitely like to appreciate the hospitality of Srayashi” – Jaikanth M

A property’s ambience is important, but what truly matters are the unique elements that create a sense of exclusivity and enhance the overall experience for guests. The Rajasthani architecture in the midst of Chola architecture seemed like a refreshing thought, and our way of standing out. The design is not the only aspect inspired by the “Land of Maharajas”. The Kalbelia dancers are one of the cultural elements we have infused, whose sway to the mesmerising Traditional Rajasthani music beats and elegance transports you to the dunes of Jaisalmer. If that’s not enough, then we are determined to craft one-of-a-kind experiences – be it a romantic dinner or a milestone event.

The testimony to this is Kaldan Samudhra’s victory of being awarded the ‘Best Property for Outdoor Weddings’ at the 10th Exotic Wedding Planning Conference (EWPC) Apex Awards 2022 held in Bangalore in Sept 2022. The Apex Awards stand as the highest form of recognition for international excellence in the Indian Wedding Industry. They are created to honor excellence and applaud exceptional achievements and contributions to the expansion of the Indian wedding sector.

Kaldan Samudhra is second to none for weddings, offering an exquisite setting for a wedding away from home. With its spectacular beach views, unique menus crafted by renowned chefs, and a 55,000 sq. Ft Lawn, Kaldan Samudhra guarantees an unforgettable experience for couples looking to exchange vows. The 10,800 sq. ft. Pillarless Grand Ballroom is ideal for hosting grand Indian and International weddings, creating a magical ambience for you and your partner. Boasting awe-inspiring beauty and world-class amenities, Kaldan Samudhra is the ideal place to make your one-of-a-kind day truly unforgettable.

Kaldan proudly reflects its commitment to delivering inspiring and memorable experiences to its guests through its range of exciting activities and events. In its very first year, from hosting teams for the Chess Olympiad 2022 to welcoming racers from the Indian Racing League to the special Cake Mixing event, Kaldan Resort has shown its dedication to creating a truly unique and memorable experience for its guests.

At the Chess Olympiad 2022, held between 27 July and 10 August, we were delighted to welcome teams from Finland, Germany, Montenegro, Iran, Hong Kong, Argentina, Portugal, and Kazakhstan. Seeing that 4 August fell within the same period, we seized the opportunity to celebrate World Chocolate Chip Cookie Day by getting the chess champs to bake Chennai’s largest cookie. With great alacrity, the guests from each of the eight countries gathered around the chef in the dining area, who then guided them through each step of the cookie-making process. Everyone was fully engaged and enthusiastic about the activity.

It was a truly extraordinary day when we had the privilege of hosting the racers of the Indian Racing League for a day of relaxation and celebration. From their arrival to their departure, we ensured it was a day they would never forget. Our guests were taken in by the rhythms of our traditional Kalbelia music and enjoyed the majestic beauty of the Sagar pool. To top it all off, we spoiled them with an array of tantalizing delicacies.

The Yuletide season at Kaldan had been eagerly anticipated for months, and when it finally arrived, it brought with it the much-awaited cake-mixing ceremony. Deliciously fragrant dry fruits, nuts, and spices were placed into a bubbling cauldron, to which bottles of wine, rum, and whisky were added. As the inviting aroma filled the air, our guests excitedly donned gloves and joined in the mixing, delighting in the sweet smells that were created.

Starting with a vision of creating a new level of luxury hospitality, the resort has implemented an array of creative and effective F&B marketing campaigns to increase its visibility and brand recognition. This year, we worked around the clock to bring you unparalleled culinary experiences – from the Biriyani Mela to the Tricolour Feast for Independence Day, from the Onam Sadhya to the Awadh Food Festival, from the Navaratri Thali to a Rajasthani food fest – ‘Ghoomar to Ghewar’. We hope that our guests enjoyed these one-of-a-kind culinary experiences!

Tamil Nadu has some of the most passionate connoisseurs of Biriyani, so it was only natural that we organised a Biriyani Mela at ‘Dhani’ – a luxurious gourmet haven that offers a unique fusion of global and Rajasthani flavours. While the Biriyani took people by storm in July, August was a season for tri-colour. The ‘Azadi ka Amrit Mahotsav’ fervour that has gripped the nation was an absolute joy for us at Kaldan as we curated the Tri-colour feast for Independence Day that included Tri-colour salads and desserts.

Inspired by the saying, ‘Love thy neighbor’, we hosted the renowned Onam Sadhya on the occasion of Onam. Authentic Kerala delicacies were curated by our chefs which touched the hearts of our guests in September. Awadh a historic region in north-central India is renowned for its unique cuisine, architecture, and music. So we wanted our guests to taste the choicest dishes of the region; as rich as its history. At the Awadh Food Festival we served kebabs, kormas, biriyanis and more.

As magnificent and vibrant as the festival of Navaratri is, so too was our Navaratri Thali an invitation for it. We crafted the thali in a manner that it was a perfect indulgence even for those fasting. In keeping with the spirit of our resort, we hosted the grandest of food festivals – ‘Ghoomar to Ghewar’. This fest was an homage to the royal culinary heritage of Rajasthan and featured special dance performances, puppet shows, and arts & crafts.

All said and done, none of this would have been possible without the patronage received from our guests. Kaldan Samudhra is our ground to deliver an upscale version of our promise of ‘Service from Heart’. We had as much fun planning every moment as attending to our guests and catering to their needs. We only hope that the coming years are grander, higher in spirits, and bring back our guests for second, third, and many more visits. Every nook and corner, every crevice of Kaldan has stories etched into it; stories of love, happiness, friendship, adventure, discovery, exploration, and magical moments.
