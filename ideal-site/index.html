01 / Since 1978 — the family side of the shore

Start as two rooms.
Grown by one family, still.

Established in 1978, Ideal Beach Resort was founded by renowned tea planter P.M. Dharmalingam. Of Sri Lankan origin, Mr Dharmalingam moved to India with a vision of delivering diverse heights of hospitality.

He embarked to Mahabalipuram — its cultural town, approachable locals and rich historical sites helped solidify the decision. Having procured 12 acres of land, Mr and Mrs Dharmalingam set about building, starting with two rooms — now known as the Garden View Rooms.

With the help of their son, Mr Bose Dharmalingam, the resort is now known as the Ideal Group of Resorts — family-run, with the proprietors personally present at both properties, and a sister resort in Thanjavur, Ideal River View Resort.

1978 — P.M. & Mrs Dharmalingam build the first two Garden View rooms on procured land.
The estate grows — more rooms, amenities, tidied gardens — at the family's own pace, on the family's own ECR acreage.
Today — son Bose Dharmalingam leads the Ideal Group: this resort plus Ideal River View Resort, Thanjavur; the proprietors present on both properties.
